Zen Mode hides the elements around the content of a page, like the header and the sidebar.

<SpeakerNotes>Switch to Zen Mode to show the difference.</SpeakerNotes>

## Entering Zen Mode

Select the Zen Mode icon in the header or in the table of contents, or use a [keyboard shortcut](/configuration/#keyboardshortcut).

Zen Mode pages are available at the URL of a page with `/zen-mode/` added after the locale, e.g. [`/zen-mode/getting-started/`](/zen-mode/getting-started/).
Links to other pages stay in Zen Mode, and switching between view modes keeps the section you are reading.

## Hidden elements

By default, Zen Mode hides the header and the sidebar.
Use the [`displayOptions`](/configuration/#displayoptions) option to choose which elements to hide.

Pages with a hero always display their header, and splash pages are not available in Zen Mode.
